# Strata Desktop

Installers for Strata, the futures strategy research app from [strataalgo.com](https://strataalgo.com).

- **Mac:** [Strata.dmg](https://github.com/hermesagent823/strata-desktop/releases/latest/download/Strata.dmg) · macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon
- **Windows:** [Strata-Setup.exe](https://github.com/hermesagent823/strata-desktop/releases/latest/download/Strata-Setup.exe) · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

After installing, sign in with your strataalgo.com account, then connect your own Databento account for market data (about two minutes; Strata shows Databento's price before anything downloads). Help: support@strataalgo.com.

This repository only hosts the installers. They contain no market data.
